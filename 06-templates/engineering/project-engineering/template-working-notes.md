# Working Notes

## Purpose

Capture the evolution of ideas, discussions, assumptions, experiments, decisions, and unresolved questions throughout the engineering lifecycle of the project.

Working Notes provide a structured record of architectural thinking, ensuring traceability, supporting reviews, and preserving the reasoning behind every major decision.
