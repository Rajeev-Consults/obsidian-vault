# Pricing & Commercials

## Purpose

This capability defines the commercial principles governing the pricing of JayaSwara services.

It establishes baseline pricing, negotiation limits, client segmentation, and commercial guidelines to ensure consistency across engagements while allowing flexibility where justified.

Project-specific quotations, proposals, and negotiated commercials are maintained within the Active-Projects vault.

---

## Scope

This capability governs:

- Pricing philosophy
- Hourly rates
- Service pricing
- Client segmentation
- Negotiation boundaries
- Commercial decision guidelines

---

## Principles

- Value before price.
- Reduce scope before reducing value.
- Long-term relationships over short-term discounts.
- Commercial consistency.
- Continuous refinement through market feedback.
