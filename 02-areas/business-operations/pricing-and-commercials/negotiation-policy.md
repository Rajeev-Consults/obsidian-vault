# Negotiation Policy

## Maximum Recommended Discounts

| Situation | Maximum Discount |
|-----------|----------------:|
| Ideal Client | 5% |
| Portfolio Opportunity | 10% |
| NGO | 15% |
| First Long-Term Client | 15% |
| Repeat Client | 10% |

---

## Principles

- Never compete solely on price.
- Reduce scope before reducing value.
- Maintain professional positioning.
- Decline engagements that fall below sustainable commercial value.
