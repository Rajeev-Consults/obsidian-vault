# Pricing Philosophy

JayaSwara pricing reflects:

- Expertise required
- Responsibility assumed
- Business value created
- Effort involved
- Complexity of engagement

Pricing is not determined solely by time spent.

Workflow Monk provides the primary estimation framework through the five engagement stages:

- Capture
- Clarify
- Organize
- Systematize
- Scale

Projects may involve one or multiple stages depending upon client requirements.
