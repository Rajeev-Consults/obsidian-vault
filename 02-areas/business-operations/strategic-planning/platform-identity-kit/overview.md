# Platform Identity Kit

## Purpose

The Platform Identity Kit is the single source of truth for JayaSwara's public professional identity.

It defines how JayaSwara is represented across freelancing platforms, professional networks, proposals, marketing assets, and future public communications.

The objective is to maintain a consistent identity while allowing platform-specific presentation where appropriate.

---

## Scope

This capability governs:

- Professional positioning
- Value proposition
- Target clients
- Service domains
- Engagement philosophy
- Workflow Monk methodology
- Portfolio categories
- Professional keywords
- Platform variants
- Signature themes

Detailed service definitions, pricing, operational procedures, and project-specific information are governed by their respective capabilities.

---

## Principles

- Single source of truth
- Consistency before customization
- Structure before marketing
- Practical over promotional
- Evolve through usage
