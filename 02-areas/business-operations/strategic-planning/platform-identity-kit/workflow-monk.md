# Workflow Monk

Workflow Monk is the consulting methodology that guides every JayaSwara engagement.

It provides a structured path from understanding an organization's current reality to building sustainable operational systems.

The methodology consists of five progressive stages:

- Capture
- Clarify
- Organize
- Systematize
- Scale

Each stage can be delivered independently or combined into broader engagements depending on the client's objectives.

The five-step progression is represented symbolically within the JayaSwara brand identity.
