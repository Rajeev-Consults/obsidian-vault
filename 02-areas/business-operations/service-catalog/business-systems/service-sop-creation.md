# Service: SOP Creation

## Purpose

To create clear, structured, and repeatable Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) that document how business processes should be executed in order to improve consistency, accountability, quality, and operational continuity.

This service transforms workflow designs into documented operational standards.

---

## Service Category

Business Systems

---

## Alignment with Jayaswara Methodology

- Systematize
    

This service focuses on documenting approved workflows and operational practices into standardized procedures before implementation, automation, or scaling activities are undertaken.

---

## Ideal Client

- Founders seeking operational consistency
    
- Businesses dependent on undocumented knowledge
    
- Growing teams requiring standardized execution
    
- Organizations preparing for delegation or scaling
    
- Businesses seeking to reduce operational dependency on individuals
    

---

## Documentation Objectives

- Standardize execution of recurring activities
    
- Improve consistency and quality of outputs
    
- Clarify responsibilities and expectations
    
- Reduce dependence on tribal knowledge
    
- Improve onboarding and knowledge transfer
    
- Improve operational continuity
    
- Support delegation and accountability
    
- Establish documented operating standards
    

---

## Scope

The engagement focuses on converting approved workflows and operational practices into structured procedural documentation.

Activities may include:

- Workflow review
    
- Procedure documentation
    
- Role and responsibility documentation
    
- Input and output definition
    
- Exception and dependency documentation
    
- Review and validation of procedures
    
- SOP standardization
    

---

## Deliverables

### Standard Operating Procedures

Documented procedures for agreed business processes.

### SOP Documentation Package

Structured documentation covering:

- Purpose
    
- Scope
    
- Roles and Responsibilities
    
- Inputs
    
- Outputs
    
- Procedure Steps
    
- Dependencies
    
- Exceptions
    
- Review Requirements
    

### SOP Review and Validation Report

Summary of stakeholder feedback, revisions, and final approvals.

---

## Scope Control

This engagement is limited to the processes, workflows, operational areas, and deliverables explicitly agreed upon during project initiation.

Requests for additional processes, expanded scope, new departments, revised objectives, or materially altered requirements may require a separate review and change request before inclusion in the engagement.

Activities, deliverables, reviews, and approvals previously completed and accepted are considered concluded unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Additional work resulting from accepted change requests may be treated as revised scope, a separate phase, or a separate engagement.

---

## Process

### Phase 1: Review

- Review workflow designs
    
- Gather process information
    
- Validate execution requirements
    
- Identify procedural dependencies
    

### Phase 2: Document

- Create SOP documentation
    
- Define roles and responsibilities
    
- Document procedures and exceptions
    
- Standardize structure and terminology
    

### Phase 3: Validate

- Review SOP drafts with stakeholders
    
- Incorporate feedback
    
- Resolve ambiguities
    
- Finalize documentation
    

### Phase 4: Approve

- Obtain stakeholder acceptance
    
- Deliver final SOP package
    
- Archive approved versions
    

---

## Acceptance Criteria

The engagement is considered complete when:

- Agreed SOPs have been documented
    
- Required stakeholder reviews have been completed
    
- Feedback has been incorporated
    
- Final SOP package has been delivered
    
- Stakeholder approval has been obtained
