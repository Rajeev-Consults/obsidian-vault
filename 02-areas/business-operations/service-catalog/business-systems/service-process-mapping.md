# Service: Process Mapping

## Purpose

To document and visualize how work currently flows through a business, team, or function in order to identify bottlenecks, inefficiencies, dependencies, risks, and opportunities for improvement.

This service helps transform operational ambiguity into operational visibility.

---

## Service Category

Business Systems

---

## Alignment with Jayaswara Methodology

- Capture
    
- Clarify
    
- Organize
    

This service focuses on understanding and documenting the current state before any redesign, systemization, automation, or scaling activities are undertaken.

---

## Ideal Client

- Solo consultants experiencing operational overload
    
- Founders managing growing teams
    
- Small and medium businesses with inconsistent execution
    
- Service businesses dependent on undocumented knowledge
    
- Organizations preparing for process improvement or systemization initiatives
    

---

## Problems Identified

- Unclear responsibilities and ownership
    
- Repeated mistakes and rework
    
- Bottlenecks in execution
    
- Dependence on tribal knowledge
    
- Dependence on legacy or manual systems
    
- Lack of visibility into operational workflows
    
- Inconsistent task execution and deliverables
    
- Difficulty onboarding new team members
    
- Unstructured business operations
    

---

## Scope

The engagement focuses on understanding and documenting existing processes.

Activities may include:

- Stakeholder interviews
    
- Process walkthrough sessions
    
- Review of existing documentation
    
- Identification of inputs, outputs, responsibilities, and dependencies
    
- Workflow visualization
    
- Bottleneck analysis
    
- Process observations and recommendations
    

---

## Deliverables

### Current-State Process Map

Visual representation of the existing workflow.

### Workflow Documentation

Documented description of process steps, roles, responsibilities, and handoffs.

### Bottleneck Analysis

Identification of inefficiencies, delays, risks, and operational friction points.

### Findings and Recommendations Report

Summary of key observations and opportunities for improvement.

### Recommendation Framework

Recommendations categorized as:

- Maintain
    
- Improve
    
- Systematize
    

to support decision-making and prioritization.

---

## Inputs Required

- Founder or stakeholder participation
    
- Access to relevant process owners
    
- Existing process documentation (if available)
    
- Relevant operational information
    
- Access to tools or systems when required
    

---

## Exclusions

This service does not include:

- Workflow redesign
    
- SOP creation
    
- Software implementation
    
- Automation implementation
    
- Team training
    
- Change management
    
- Project management
    
- AI integration
    
- Knowledge management system setup
    

These may be addressed through separate services.

---

## Process

### Phase 1: Capture

- Stakeholder discussions
    
- Information gathering
    
- Documentation review
    
- Process discovery
    

### Phase 2: Clarify

- Process analysis
    
- Bottleneck identification
    
- Dependency mapping
    
- Responsibility mapping
    

### Phase 3: Organize

- Workflow visualization
    
- Documentation preparation
    
- Findings consolidation
    
- Recommendation development
    

---

## Recommendation Framework

### Maintain

Current process is acceptable and does not require immediate intervention.

### Improve

Targeted improvements are recommended to address specific inefficiencies with minimal disruption.

### Systematize

Significant redesign, documentation, implementation, automation, or systemization is recommended to improve performance and scalability.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

The engagement is considered complete when:

- Agreed processes have been documented
    
- Current-state workflows have been visualized
    
- Major bottlenecks have been identified
    
- Findings and recommendations have been delivered
    
- Final deliverables have been shared with the client
    

---

## Outcomes

Clients gain:

- Clear visibility into how work currently happens
    
- Better understanding of operational dependencies
    
- Identification of process inefficiencies
    
- Foundation for future systemization and scaling initiatives
    
- Improved operational awareness
