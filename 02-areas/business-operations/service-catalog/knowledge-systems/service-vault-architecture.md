# Service Vault Architecture

## Purpose

Define where service-related knowledge is stored, maintained, and retrieved within the Jayaswara Professional Vault.

This note provides placement rules, ownership boundaries, and navigation principles so that every knowledge asset has a clear home and can be located efficiently.

---

# Core Principle

Architecture serves work.

Work does not serve architecture.

The vault exists to support execution, delivery, learning, and business growth. Structural changes should only be made when they improve clarity, reduce friction, or support scale.

---

# Architectural Principles

## One Clear Home

Every note should have one primary location.

Navigation should be achieved through links and MOCs rather than duplicate notes.

---

## Projects Are Temporary

Projects have a beginning and end.

Completed project knowledge should be retained only when it remains operationally useful.

---

## Areas Are Ongoing

Areas represent continuing responsibilities, capabilities, and systems that require ongoing maintenance.

---

## Resources Support Execution

Resources exist to support services, operations, decision-making, content creation, and learning.

---

## Channel Agnostic By Default

Systems, frameworks, SOPs, templates, and workflows should support remote, in-person, and hybrid delivery unless channel-specific requirements materially affect execution.

---

## Progress Over Perfection

Implement first.

Refine through use.

Architectural decisions should be validated through practical application.
