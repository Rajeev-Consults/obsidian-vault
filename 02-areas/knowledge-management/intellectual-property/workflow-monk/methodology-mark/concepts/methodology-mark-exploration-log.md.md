# Workflow Monk Methodology Mark — Exploration Log

## Purpose

This document records all methodology mark explorations, observations, evaluations, and decisions.

The objective is not to find a mark quickly.

The objective is to identify a symbol capable of becoming the long-term visual identity of Workflow Monk.

Review this document regularly with fresh eyes.

Allow concepts to mature before making final decisions.

---

# Status Definitions

## Considered

Interesting concept.

Worth further exploration.

Insufficient confidence for shortlisting.

---

## Maybe

Strong potential.

Should remain active for continued evaluation.

Requires refinement or comparison.

---

## Shortlisted

High confidence candidate.

Represents the philosophy effectively.

Deserves detailed evaluation.

---

## Rejected

No longer aligned with the methodology vision.

Retained only for future reference and learning.

---

# Evaluation Criteria

Score mentally against:

- Memorability
    
- Simplicity
    
- Distinctiveness
    
- Timelessness
    
- Scalability
    
- Platform Independence
    
- Alignment With Workflow Monk Character
    
- Suitability For Long-Term Use
