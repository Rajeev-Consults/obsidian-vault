# Vault Coherence Review

## Purpose

Ensure that the JayaSwara knowledge ecosystem remains coherent, connected, navigable, maintainable, and aligned with its intended architecture.

The objective is not to create more notes.

The objective is to ensure that existing notes continue to support execution, learning, delivery, content creation, intellectual property development, and business growth.

---

# Review Frequency

## Launch Phase

Weekly

---

## Operational Phase

Monthly

---

## Major Events

Perform an additional review after:

- Major vault restructuring
    
- New service launches
    
- New methodology releases
    
- Significant content expansion
    
- Completion of client projects
    
- Quarterly business reviews
    

---

# Review Areas

## 1. Vault Role Alignment

Verify that each vault continues to serve its intended purpose.

### ObsidianVault

Business operating system.

Contains:

- Services
    
- Methodologies
    
- Intellectual Property
    
- Website
    
- Brand Assets
    
- CRM
    
- Operations
    
- Content
    

### Active-Projects

Execution vault.

Contains:

- Active client work
    
- Current projects
    
- Working notes
    
- Project resources
    

### Knowledge Vault

Long-term memory.

Contains:

- Consolidated learning
    
- Mature frameworks
    
- Archived research
    
- Historical reference material
    

---

## 2. Orphan Notes Review

Identify notes with:

- No inbound links
    
- No outbound links
    
- No MOC references
    

Determine:

- Link
    
- Merge
    
- Archive
    
- Delete
    

---

## 3. MOC Coverage Review

Verify important notes appear within relevant MOCs.

Examples:

- Services → Services MOC
    
- Website → Website MOC
    
- Workflow Monk → Workflow Monk MOC
    
- Operations → Operations MOC
    

---

## 4. Knowledge Flow Review

Validate flow:

Research  
→ Framework  
→ Service  
→ Delivery Process  
→ Project  
→ Insight  
→ Framework Improvement

Identify broken transitions.

---

## 5. Project Extraction Review

Review completed work.

Extract:

- Lessons Learned
    
- Patterns
    
- Reusable Assets
    
- SOP Candidates
    
- Framework Improvements
    

Move findings into Insights.

---

## 6. Navigation Review

Check:

- MOCs
    
- Dashboards
    
- Service references
    
- Framework references
    
- Cross-domain links
    

Goal:

Find important information within three clicks.

---

## 7. Empty Notes Review

Review placeholder notes.

Decision:

- Populate
    
- Merge
    
- Archive
    
- Delete
    

Avoid structural clutter.

---

## 8. Knowledge Vault Migration Review

Determine whether information should be migrated to Knowledge Vault.

Candidates:

- Mature frameworks
    
- Consolidated research
    
- Historical reference material
    
- Reusable knowledge assets
    

---

## 9. Workflow Monk Alignment Review

Verify consistency between:

- Workflow Monk
    
- Service Catalog
    
- Website
    
- Brand Positioning
    
- Content
    

Identify divergence.

---

### Findings
