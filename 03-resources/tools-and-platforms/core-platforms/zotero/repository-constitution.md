## Purpose

Define the constitutional role, responsibilities, and boundaries of the knowledge repository.

---

## Scope

Applies to all knowledge assets managed by the repository.

---

## Constitutional Role

The repository serves as the authoritative reference system for curated knowledge assets.

It preserves, organizes, and provides access to knowledge while supporting learning, research, consulting, and content development.

---

## Responsibilities

- Preserve knowledge assets.
- Maintain bibliographic integrity.
- Support discovery and retrieval.
- Enable consistent organization.
- Support downstream knowledge systems.

---

## Boundaries

The repository does not manage:

- Projects
- Tasks
- Operational workflows
- Temporary working material
- Knowledge synthesis

These responsibilities belong to other systems.

---

## Constitutional Principle

The repository preserves knowledge.

Other systems create value from knowledge.
