## Purpose

Define the physical organization and storage principles governing repository assets.

---

## Scope

Applies to bibliographic records, attachments, notes, metadata, and supporting resources.

---

## Principles

- Separate storage from organization.
- Preserve original assets.
- Maintain stable storage locations.
- Minimize duplication.
- Support backup and recovery.

---

## Design Objective

Provide a portable, maintainable, and resilient storage architecture.
