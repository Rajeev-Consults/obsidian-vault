# Metadata Standards

## Purpose

Define the metadata principles governing the documentation of technology capabilities within the JayaSwara ecosystem.

---

## Scope

Applies to all technology capabilities documented under the Tools and Platforms domain, irrespective of implementation platform, vendor, or technology.

---

## Principles

- Metadata shall support identification, organization, navigation, and governance.
- Metadata shall remain consistent across comparable capability documentation.
- Metadata shall complement document content rather than duplicate it.
- Metadata shall evolve through review and governance.
- Metadata shall remain independent of implementation technologies wherever practical.

---

## Required Metadata

Every capability note shall include:

- Document Identity
- Purpose
- Scope
- Version Information
- Owner
- Related Notes

---

## Optional Metadata

Additional metadata may be introduced where justified by capability-specific requirements, provided consistency is maintained within the capability.

---

## Design Objective

Establish a consistent metadata foundation that improves discoverability, maintainability, governance, and long-term evolution across technology capability documentation.
