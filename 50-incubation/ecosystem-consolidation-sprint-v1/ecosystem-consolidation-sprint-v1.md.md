- Evidence

It will begin by determining:

# 4. Stage Completion Protocol

A stage is not considered complete merely because its tasks are checked.

The stage must pass four gates:

### Gate 1 — Work Complete

- Required tasks completed
    
- Required assets created
    
- Required gaps addressed
    

### Gate 2 — Audit

- Results reviewed
    
- Duplicates resolved
    
- Scope boundaries confirmed
    
- Evidence checked
    

### Gate 3 — Approval

- Result is stable enough for V1
    
- Claims are supported
    
- Delivery is credible
    
- Reuse is practical
    

### Gate 4 — Promotion

- Approved result moved/promoted to its permanent logical home
    
- Master note updated
    
- Stage marked complete
    
- Next stage released

# 5. Workflow Monk Operating Layer

Workflow Monk is not a separate project.

It is the natural operating behaviour of the ecosystem.

```
CAPTURE
   ↓
CLARIFY
   ↓
ORGANIZE
   ↓
SYSTEMATIZE
   ↓
SCALE
```

This should increasingly become implicit in how internal and commercial projects are handled.

### Capture

Make information, requirements, processes and observations visible.

### Clarify

Understand what exists, what matters and what is missing.

### Organize

Give information and assets a useful structure.

### Systematize

Turn useful knowledge into repeatable ways of working.

### Scale

Reuse, automate, publish, deliver and improve.

# 6. Knowledge / EKAR Principle

The 1,500+ book library and EKAR ecosystem are supporting infrastructure for the sprint.

They are **not a separate reading project**.

```
CAPABILITY GAP
      ↓
EKAR DISCOVERY
      ↓
RELEVANT KNOWLEDGE
      ↓
TARGETED READING
      ↓
PRACTICE
      ↓
CAPABILITY
```

### Rule

> **Read to close capability gaps, not to accumulate knowledge.**

---

# 7. Promotion Principle

Nothing is promoted simply because it exists.

Promote only when:

- It has been worked through
    
- It has been reviewed
    
- It is sufficiently stable
    
- It is evidence-backed
    
- Its logical home is known
    
- It can be reused
    
- It does not create unnecessary duplication
    
- It represents an approved V1 state
