# Purpose

Define the constitutional principles governing all work performed within the incubation environment.

These principles guide architectural thinking, knowledge engineering, reviews, and promotion while ensuring consistency across the JayaSwara ecosystem.

---

# Principles

## 1. Process before Platform

Design the process before selecting or implementing technology.

---

## 2. Workflow before Software

Workflows define software requirements, not the other way around.

---

## 3. Structure before Automation

Organize knowledge before attempting to automate it.

---

## 4. Capability before Implementation

Define organizational capabilities independently of the technologies used to implement them.

---

## 5. Simplicity before Complexity

Prefer simple, understandable solutions over sophisticated but unnecessary designs.

---

## 6. Technology Neutrality

Architectural decisions should remain valid despite changes in software, AI models, or platforms.

---

## 7. Single Responsibility

Every note, folder, template, capability, and organizational component should have one primary responsibility.

---

## 8. Traceability

All significant architectural decisions should remain traceable to their origin.

---

## 9. Reuse before Reinvention

Promote reusable organizational assets whenever possible.

---

## 10. Human where Valuable

Automation should augment human judgment rather than replace it.

---

## 11. Continuous Improvement

Architectural standards evolve through review, practical application, and controlled refinement.

---

## 12. Promotion through Validation

Knowledge becomes organizational knowledge only after successful review and acceptance.

---

## Guiding Philosophy

The incubation environment exists to transform ideas into validated organizational capabilities through disciplined engineering, structured governance, and continuous learning.
