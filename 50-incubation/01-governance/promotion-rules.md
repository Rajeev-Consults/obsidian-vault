# Purpose

Define the governance rules for promoting knowledge from incubation into the permanent JayaSwara knowledge ecosystem.

---

# Promotion Principles

- Promotion follows validation.
- Promotion is evidence-based.
- Promotion is reversible.
- Promotion preserves traceability.
- Promotion favors reuse.

---

# Promotion Requirements

Before promotion, an initiative should have:

- Completed Overview
- Working Notes consolidated
- References verified
- Architecture Review completed
- Human Review completed
- Version assigned

---

# Promotion Levels

## Section Promotion

Approved sections may be promoted independently.

---

## Document Promotion

Entire documents may be promoted when all required sections have been accepted.

---

## Template Promotion

Engineering or operational templates become organizational standards after successful review.

---

# Promotion Destinations

Possible destinations include:

- 01-projects
- 02-areas
- 03-resources
- 05-operations
- 06-templates
- 07-insights
- 08-content

---

# Versioning

Promotion creates a stable organizational version.

Future improvements begin a new incubation cycle.

---

# Traceability

Every promoted artifact should maintain links to:

- Originating initiative
- Architecture Review
- Version history
- Supporting references
