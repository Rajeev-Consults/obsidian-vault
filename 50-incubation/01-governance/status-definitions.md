# Purpose

Define the standard lifecycle states used throughout the incubation environment.

These definitions provide consistent interpretation across projects, governance, automation, and reporting.

---

# Lifecycle States

## Draft

Initial creation.

Ideas are actively evolving.

---

## Active

Work is currently in progress.

---

## Architecture Review

The initiative is undergoing architectural evaluation.

---

## Human Review

Architectural recommendations are being evaluated by the human reviewer.

---

## Revision

Changes are being incorporated following review.

---

## Accepted

The current version has been approved for organizational use.

---

## Promoted

Approved knowledge has been incorporated into its permanent location within the main vault.

---

## Versioned

A stable version has been formally recorded.

---

## Maintained

The artifact is operational and subject to periodic review.

---

## Archived

The artifact has been superseded, retired, or preserved for historical reference.

---

# Transition Principles

Lifecycle states should progress through review rather than bypass governance.

Promotion represents organizational adoption rather than document completion.

Version history preserves architectural evolution.
